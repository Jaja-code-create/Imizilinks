## The principle in one sentence

Social security reimburses part of your healthcare costs. Top-up insurance, called a mutuelle, covers all or part of the rest. The two are separate and you apply for each one separately.

## Step 1, register with social security

If you are an international student, you register online on the website for international students. If you have come to work, your employer starts the registration, but you still need to complete your file.

The documents usually requested

- An identity document or your passport
- Your residence permit or validated visa
- Your birth certificate translated into French
- French bank details (RIB)
- Proof of enrolment or your employment contract

> **Get your birth certificate translated early.** It is the document that holds up the most applications. If you can, have it translated by a certified translator before you even leave.

## Step 2, your temporary number, then your permanent one

You first receive a temporary number, which already allows you to be reimbursed. Your permanent fifteen-digit number comes later. Do not worry about the wait, as any care in the meantime is reimbursed retroactively.

## Step 3, the carte Vitale

You apply for your carte Vitale from your Ameli account once your permanent number has been issued. It means you do not have to pay upfront at most practitioners.

Until it arrives, keep all your paper treatment forms, which let you claim reimbursement afterwards.

## Step 4, register a regular doctor

Your médecin traitant is your way into the system. If you see a specialist without going through them, you are reimbursed less.

Choose a GP near where you live, book a first appointment and fill in the registration form together. It is free and takes five minutes.

## Step 5, top-up health insurance

Social security does not cover everything. A mutuelle pays part of the rest, especially for glasses, dental care and hospital stays.

- If you work, your employer offers one, often at a good rate.
- If you are a student, there are low-cost offers designed for you.
- If your income is low, you may be entitled to the complémentaire santé solidaire, which is free or very cheap.

## In an emergency before you are registered

Hospital emergency departments never turn anyone away. You will receive a bill, but it can be settled once your registration is complete. Never go without urgent care because your file has not been approved yet.

## Good habits

- Create your Ameli account as soon as you have your number, as you can track everything there.
- Keep all your documents for at least two years.
- Report any change of address or circumstances.

Procedures and amounts change over time. Always check the information on official websites before taking any step.
